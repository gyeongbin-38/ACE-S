# TRUST

Use when the next decision depends on identity, privilege, tenant/resource isolation, secret ownership, authorization, audit, or regulatory security boundaries.

Check first:
- who is the principal?
- what resource/action is authorized?
- where is the decision enforced?
- what is the least-privilege capability/credential scope?
- what blast radius follows credential/component compromise?

Do not load for generic security wording with no architecture-changing trust decision.

If material, read `../references/trust-security.md`.
