# Evidence and Provenance

Use for high-stakes, disputed, exact, or externally sourced claims.

Prefer evidence that is:
- direct,
- current enough,
- scoped to the requested population or artifact,
- independently corroborated when necessary,
- traceable to an exact source.

When two sources conflict, do not average them. Compare ownership, freshness, definition, scope, and directness.

For exact wording, contracts, numbers, or changed requirements, re-open the raw source before finalizing.
